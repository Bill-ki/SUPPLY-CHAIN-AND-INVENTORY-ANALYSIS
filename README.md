# Supply Chain & Inventory Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses inventory operations and supplier performance within a retail and distribution business. It focuses on stock availability, inventory valuation, reorder levels, stock movements, discontinued products, and supplier-related performance indicators.

## Analysis Performed
- Evaluated inventory levels and stock valuation.
- Identified products at or below their reorder levels.
- Analysed discontinued products and their associated inventory value.
- Examined restocking, adjustments, damaged stock, and sales-related inventory movements.
- Reviewed supplier performance and damage patterns.

## Key Findings
- Eight products are at or below their reorder levels.
- Three discontinued products account for approximately $124,362 in inventory value.
- Continental Fabrics recorded a damage rate of approximately 21.6% on restocked units.
- Inventory records include 169 restocks, 68 adjustments, 62 damaged movements, and 51 sales-related movements.
